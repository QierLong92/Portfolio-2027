UX/UI Design · 2026

Saline Music Repair

A clearer, calmer service experience for a local instrument repair business.为本地乐器维修店打造更清晰、更安心的服务体验。

Client客户
Saline Music Repair
Role职责
UX DesignerUX 设计师
Timeline周期
Mar–Apr 2026
Focus重点
Service clarity服务透明度

The opportunity设计机会

Make every step of repair visible.让维修过程的每一步都清晰可见。

Instrument repair often depends on fragmented calls, unclear pricing, and uncertain pickup timing. Saline brings inquiries, a service-cost guide, repair tracking, payments, and pickup logistics into one mobile platform.乐器维修常常依赖零散的电话沟通、模糊的价格信息与不确定的取件时间。Saline 将咨询、服务报价、维修追踪、付款与取件信息整合进一个移动平台。

Key flows核心流程

One service, from request to pickup.从维修申请到取件的一体化服务。

01

Start a repair request发起维修申请

Instrument search and structured service options help customers describe what they need without relying on a phone call.乐器搜索与结构化服务选项帮助用户清楚描述需求，减少对电话沟通的依赖。

02

See the work in progress查看维修进度

A task-based status view keeps repair details, technician contact, and updates visible throughout the service journey.基于任务的状态页面将维修详情、技师联系方式与进度更新集中呈现。

03

Pay with confidence透明费用与付款

Itemized repair and storage fees make every charge clear before checkout, building trust in a traditionally opaque process.维修费与保管费逐项展示，让用户在付款前清楚了解每一项费用。

Final interface最终界面

Designed for clarity at a glance.一眼看清每一个关键信息。

A consistent system connects home, status, service selection, billing, and payment screens.统一的设计系统连接首页、进度、服务选择、账单与付款页面。

Outcome成果

A repair service that feels as carefully handled as the instrument itself.像对待乐器一样，细致地对待每一次维修服务。
