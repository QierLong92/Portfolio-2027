Kelsey Dex

A museum artifact Pokédex that turns a visit to ancient Rome into a collection-driven adventure.一款博物馆文物图鉴应用，把古罗马展览变成一场以收藏为驱动的探索冒险。

Timeline周期

The opportunity设计机会

Make museum learning feel like discovery.让博物馆学习重新拥有发现感。

Traditional exhibit labels can make Roman history feel distant and passive. Kelsey Dex gives visitors a reason to move, look closely, and build a collection. Each scan reveals an artifact, a 3D experience, and a piece of a character’s everyday story.传统展签容易让罗马历史显得遥远而被动。Kelsey Dex 鼓励参观者移动、观察并建立自己的收藏。每次扫描都会解锁一件文物、一次 3D 体验，以及一个历史人物日常故事的片段。

The experience体验流程

A guided journey from curiosity to connection.从好奇到连接的引导式旅程。

01

Scan artifacts to unlock the world扫描文物，解锁探索世界

The scanner turns the physical exhibition into the first interaction. Visitors point their camera at an artifact to unlock the introduction and historical characters.扫描器把实体展览变成第一次互动。参观者将相机对准文物，解锁介绍内容与历史人物。

02

Learn, quiz, and follow the collection学习、答题并继续探索收藏

Artifact descriptions pair origin maps and quizzes with related objects, giving visitors a clear next step and a reason to keep looking.文物说明将来源地图与答题结合，并推荐相关藏品，让参观者知道下一步去哪里、继续寻找什么。

03

Meet the character behind the objects认识文物背后的历史人物

Character pages connect individual artifacts to a person and their everyday life, making the collection feel like a story rather than a list of objects.人物页面将一件件文物连接到具体人物与日常生活，让收藏成为故事，而不只是物品清单。

04

Unlock the life story解锁人物生活故事

Collecting enough related artifacts reveals a comic about the character’s life, rewarding curiosity with a memorable narrative.集齐足够的相关文物后，应用会解锁人物生活漫画，用一段难忘的叙事奖励持续探索。

05

Load the artifact database加载文物数据库

A searchable artifact database keeps the experience useful beyond the collection game, with clear metadata, images, and object context.可浏览的文物数据库让体验不止于收藏游戏，通过清晰的元数据、图片与背景信息持续支持学习。

Collect to reveal收藏并解锁

Every artifact opens another story.每件文物都打开一段新的故事。

When a character’s complete set is collected, visitors unlock a comic about Roman daily life—and a reason to keep exploring.集齐一个角色的文物后，参观者将解锁一篇关于罗马日常生活的漫画，也获得继续探索的理由。

Making the prototype原型制作

A collaborative system for an interactive museum.为互动博物馆体验搭建协作系统。

Working with a four-person UX/UI team, I contributed to sketches, interface prototypes, and user interviews with museum stakeholders. We used Figma Make to explore the front-end experience and 8th Wall to prototype artifact models and AR interactions.我与四人 UX/UI 团队共同完成草图、界面原型，并通过与博物馆相关方访谈持续优化收集式玩法。我们使用 Figma Make 探索前端体验，并使用 8th Wall 制作文物模型与 AR 交互原型。

The result connects the physical exhibition to a digital story layer without asking visitors to stop being curious. The interface stays light and playful, while the content gives each object a human context.最终体验将实体展览与数字故事层连接起来，同时保留参观者主动探索的乐趣。界面轻盈而有趣，内容则为每件文物补充了具有人情味的背景。

Outcome成果

A museum visit that continues after the scan.一次扫描之后，博物馆体验仍在继续。
