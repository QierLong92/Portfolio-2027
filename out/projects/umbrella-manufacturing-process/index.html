Umbrella Manufacturing Process雨伞制造工艺

An illustrated guide that turns the hidden materials, mechanisms, and sequence behind an everyday umbrella into a clear visual story.将一把日常雨伞背后的材料、机械结构与生产步骤，转化为清晰易读的视觉叙事。

The brief设计命题

Reveal the system inside a familiar object.揭示熟悉物件之中的系统。

An umbrella looks simple when it is open or folded away, yet it is the result of a precise assembly of canopy, ribs, runners, shaft, and handle. This project maps those relationships and makes each transition in the manufacturing process visible, one illustrated step at a time.雨伞在打开或收起时看似简单，却由伞面、伞骨、中巢、伞杆与手柄等精密部件共同构成。本项目梳理这些部件之间的关系，并以逐步插画呈现制造过程中的每一次转变。

Process sequence工艺流程

A ten-step path from material to mechanism.从材料到机械结构的十个步骤。

The circular illustrations work as a visual index: each frame isolates one operation, while the sequence explains how the umbrella gradually takes form.圆形插画构成视觉索引：每一帧聚焦一个操作步骤，串联起来则呈现雨伞如何逐渐成形。

Assembly logic组装逻辑

See the parts, then understand the whole.先看见部件，再理解整体。

Structural diagrams shift scale between individual components and their connections, showing why sequence matters as much as form.结构图在单个部件与它们的连接关系之间切换尺度，说明了为何制造顺序与最终形态同样重要。

Information as narrative信息即叙事

Make a complex sequence feel tangible.让复杂步骤变得可感知。

The visual system balances technical clarity with a human scale. Neutral tones and simplified forms keep attention on the relationships between tools, materials, and motions rather than overwhelming the reader with detail.视觉系统在技术清晰度与亲和尺度之间取得平衡。中性色调与简化造型引导读者关注工具、材料与动作之间的关系，而非被过多细节淹没。
