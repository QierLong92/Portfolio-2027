Vibrant: The Third Place

A public-transit campaign that makes body language, shared space, and everyday connection more visible.一项通过公共交通空间，让肢体语言、共享边界与日常连接变得更可见的设计活动。

The opportunity设计机会

Public transit is more than the space between home and work.公共交通不只是家与工作之间的通道。

Words are only part of how people communicate. In the crowded, mobile setting of public transit, posture, proximity, and small gestures can shape whether a ride feels comfortable or awkward. Vibrant helps passengers notice these unintentional signals and practice a more considerate shared experience.语言只是沟通的一部分。在拥挤而流动的公共交通中，姿态、距离与细微动作都会影响一段旅程是舒适还是尴尬。Vibrant 让乘客留意这些无意识的信号，并练习更体贴的共享体验。

Campaign system活动系统

From a private reaction to a shared action.从个人感受走向共享行动。

01

Listen before designing从观察开始设计

A cultural-probe activity invited riders to recall moments when another person’s body language changed the meaning of an interaction. The responses made subtle, unintentional gestures tangible.通过文化探针活动，邀请乘客回忆他人的肢体语言如何改变一次互动的感受，让无意识的非语言表达变得可见。

02

Turn transit into a third place让公共交通成为第三空间

A playful poster series reframes the bus or subway as a shared social setting—one where awareness of posture, distance, and gesture can make a ride feel more welcoming.海报将公交和地铁重新定义为共享的社交场所，让乘客意识到姿态、距离和动作如何创造更友好的乘车体验。

03

Carry the conversation forward让对话延伸到旅程之外

Pocket-sized brochures introduce the campaign, explain its activities, and give passengers a take-away prompt for noticing their own role in the public space.便携折页介绍活动与设计意图，并以互动提示引导乘客关注自己在公共空间中的角色。

Poster series海报系统

A visual language built for the journey.为旅程而生的视觉语言。

Bold color fields, warm gestures, and direct prompts make the campaign legible in a fast-moving transit environment.明快色彩、温暖动作与直接提示，让活动在快速流动的交通环境中依然清晰可读。

In context落地场景

Designed to meet people where the ride happens.在真实乘车场景中与乘客相遇。

The system spans station screens, interior advertisements, posters, and brochures—multiple touchpoints that invite participation without interrupting the ride.系统涵盖站内屏幕、车厢广告、海报与折页，以多种触点邀请参与，同时不打扰乘客的旅程。
