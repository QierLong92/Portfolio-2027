UX/UI Design · 2025

NYPD Website Redesign

A more inviting digital front door for a neighborhood pizza favorite.为社区披萨店打造更亲切、更好用的数字门面。

Client客户
New York Pizza Depot
Role职责
UX/UI DesignerUX/UI 设计师
Format形式
Responsive website响应式网站
Focus重点
Ordering clarity点单体验

The opportunity设计机会

Make a local favorite easier to discover and order.让本地人喜爱的披萨店更容易被发现，也更容易下单。

The redesign brings the restaurant’s personality, menu, and ordering flow into one responsive system. It uses a bold brick-red and sunny-yellow palette to feel familiar, while clearer hierarchy helps customers move from appetite to checkout.这次改版将餐厅个性、菜单与下单流程整合进一个响应式系统。大胆的砖红与明黄色延续品牌熟悉感，清晰的信息层级则帮助用户从产生食欲顺畅走到结账。

Key flows核心流程

A responsive experience built around real decisions.围绕真实点单决策构建的响应式体验。

01

A warmer first impression更有温度的第一印象

A bold hero and promotional content make the local restaurant feel recognizable before a customer starts ordering.醒目的首屏与促销内容，让用户在开始下单前就能感受到餐厅的本地特色。

02

Browse the menu with confidence更清晰地浏览菜单

A category-led menu gives a broad selection of food a simple, scannable structure.以品类为核心的菜单结构，让丰富的食物选择更容易浏览和理解。

03

Make customization feel manageable让个性化点单更易操作

Step-by-step options turn a complex pizza order into a clear sequence of decisions.分步骤的选项将复杂的披萨定制拆解为清晰、易完成的选择。

Responsive system响应式系统

One visual language, from desktop to phone.从桌面端到手机端，保持统一的视觉语言。

The same hierarchy adapts to different contexts without losing the restaurant’s character.统一的信息层级适配不同使用场景，同时保留餐厅独有的品牌个性。

Outcome成果

A digital storefront that feels as lively as the neighborhood it serves.一个和所在社区一样有活力的数字门店。
